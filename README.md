# WOFU 🛒
# We Order For U
### Global Shopping for Zambia

WOFU is a Zambia-focused cross-border shopping platform designed to make it easier for customers in Zambia to purchase products from international marketplaces.

Instead of navigating international marketplaces, payment systems, shipping arrangements, and customs processes on their own, customers will be able to submit a product request through WOFU and receive a local quotation.

---

## 🚀 How WOFU Works

1. 🛍️ **Product Request**  
   Customer submits a product link or product request.

2. 💰 **Admin Quote**  
   WOFU calculates the product, shipping, service, and other applicable costs.

3. 💳 **Local Payment**  
   Customer pays locally in Zambian Kwacha.

4. 🌍 **International Purchase**  
   WOFU purchases the product from the international marketplace.

5. 🚢 **International Shipping**  
   The product is shipped to Zambia.

6. 📦 **Zambia Delivery**  
   Customer receives the product and can track the order.

🎯 Project Goal

The goal of WOFU is to simplify international shopping for customers in Zambia by bringing product ordering, pricing, payment, shipping, and delivery into one platform.

🛠️ Project Status

Currently in development.

The project is being developed incrementally, starting with the core application foundation before introducing marketplace integrations, payments, order management, shipping, and other features.
